Recommended use

Keep the concepts separate

Use population as the denominator. For the healthcare question, foreign_nationals is the closest citizenship-based measure where covered; use foreign_born as a clearly labelled robustness alternative. Irregular stocks, overstayer proxies, and enforcement detections are not pooled into a single construct.
